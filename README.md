# Do You Have The GUTS? 2026

Our entry to the GUTS hackathon for the JPMorganChase 2 button game challenge.
## Team 59 AI Report

### AI Usage scale 5
This is a vibe coded game. We did not write code, beyond modifying a couple of specific values. Everything was designed and created with intention and clear image in mind.

**Models/subs used:**
- Claude Pro Subscription
- Opus 5.5
- Sonnet 5.5
- Codex
- 6.1 Sol
### Description

We first went through the basics of what a two button game could look like. We thought of various ideas: decision tree games, Mario party style virtual board games, then various mini game style games.

We landed on a mechanic of continuous movement and steering with the two buttons. Then the idea of someone on an office chair with a fire extinguisher to propel them. Inspired by Brooklyn 99.

We then compared that way of moving with a two sided booster system and momentum, but landed on single constant power with steering. Then we thought about map design, office chairs in an office. Different office environments, mainly pun inspired.

AI entered at this stage where we went on to build out gameplay from there, by prompting models with clear map design and mechanic features. We did a lot of thorough testing and refinement, which we believe has made a really fun game.

I hope you enjoy!
